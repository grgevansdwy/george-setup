# Design: <project name>

## 1. Problem and assumptions
What I understood the task to be, and what I assumed where the spec was vague.

## 2. Architecture
Diagram (image or ASCII) plus 3-5 sentences on the data flow, from query to answer.

## 3. Key decisions and why
Orchestration approach, how tools are described and selected, how external
data/tool integrations (MCP, RAG) fit in. Each decision gets one line of
reasoning and the alternative I rejected.

## 4. Failure handling and limits
Retries, fallbacks, step and time bounds, what the system says when it can't answer.

## 5. Testing
Queries I tried (simple, multi-tool, failure, out-of-scope) and what happened.

## 6. Tradeoffs, limitations, next steps
What I cut because of the 4-hour limit, known weaknesses, what I'd build next.
